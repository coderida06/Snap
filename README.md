#SNAP
The Snap was the moment Thanos wiped out half the world's population with one snap. Fortunately, we weren't in the Marvel Universe to experience it (phew).

Then came Iron Man with his iconic "I am Iron Man." snap, bringing everyone back. This became known as The Blip, and unfortunately, we weren't in the Marvel Universe to experience that😔.

Maybe we'll never get to visit the Marvel Universe... unless.... Spider-Man opens up a portal between our universe and theirs. But its a long shot. So... I made Snap!

Snap lets you experience being Thanos, wiping out an entire city, and then becoming Iron Man to bring everyone back like the hero you are.

 Features:
🫰 Real-time hand gesture recognition
💀 Become Thanos and erase half the city
❤️ Become Iron Man and bring everyone back
🌆 Animated city with particle effects
🎨 Two completely different UI themes
📷 Live webcam feed
⚡ Smooth animations and optimized rendering
🖥️ Desktop application built with CustomTkinter


 Built With:
Python
OpenCV
MediaPipe
CustomTkinter
Pillow
NumPy


 How it Works:
Launch the application.
Pick your destiny.
💀 Thanos
❤️ Iron Man
Show your hand to the webcam.
Pinch your thumb and middle finger together (basically telling you to snap your fingers).
Release.

Sit back and watch the city change.
